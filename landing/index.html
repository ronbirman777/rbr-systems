About Me
Bridging consciousness & technology

For the past three years, I've been managing a retreat center in Thailand, while also producing retreats of my own and working closely with facilitators, teachers, and groups from around the world.

That experience showed me a gap that kept appearing again and again. Retreats are deeply human experiences, but much of what surrounds them, including schedules, updates, resources, communication, and guest information, is still often fragmented or unnecessarily complicated.

Guests shouldn't need to search for a printed schedule or ask the same practical questions throughout the day. They should be able to stay present, connect more deeply with their teacher or facilitator, and access everything that supports their journey without technology getting in the way.

At the same time, facilitators shouldn't have to spend their energy repeatedly sharing information, managing scattered resources, or answering questions that could be handled beautifully and instantly.

That's why I created RBR.

I build digital experiences that bridge those two worlds. Guests get a simple, intuitive way to experience the retreat at its best, while facilitators and retreat centers get an effortless way to organize and share everything their participants need.

Technology shouldn't compete with the retreat experience. It should quietly support it. Simple enough to disappear into the background, thoughtful enough to make everything easier, and beautifully integrated into the experience itself.
