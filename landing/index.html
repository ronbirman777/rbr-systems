About Me
Bridging consciousness & technology

RBR exists to close the gap between the depth of a retreat experience and the clarity of modern technology. Guests shouldn't need a printed schedule taped to a dining hall door, and facilitators shouldn't need to answer the same question five times a day.

Having spent time around retreat centers, yoga schools and healing spaces, I've learned what actually matters on the ground — a schedule people trust, a way to reach their teacher, a library they keep returning to long after they've gone home.

I build digital experiences that support that rhythm instead of competing with it: simple enough to disappear into the background, beautiful enough to feel like part of the space itself.
