# RBR — Instagram Content Plan

## Positioning
**RBR · DataFrame & Systems Solutions** — boutique internal software for hospitality & wellness businesses: guest apps, staff tools, booking sites, and point-of-sale/back-office systems, built and branded per property (not off-the-shelf SaaS).

**Target audience:** owners/operators of retreats, boutique hotels, spas and wellness resorts who are running operations on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and paper checklists, and are losing time/money to it.

## Content pillars
| Pillar | % | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Case studies (carousels) | 40% | The 4 product carousels below — one system per post |
| Behind-the-build | 25% | Short clips/screens of a feature being designed, before/after of a manual process |
| Educational | 20% | "3 signs your ops team needs a real system," "spreadsheet vs. app" comparisons |
| Social proof | 10% | Quote graphics from the testimonials, once real client quotes exist |
| Offer/CTA | 5% | Direct "book a call" / "DM us" posts |
